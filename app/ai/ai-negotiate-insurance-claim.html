What AI Negotiation Support Does

Claim Command Pro's AI negotiation tools analyze the gap between the insurer's offer and your documented loss, determine the most effective negotiation strategy for your specific situation, and generate a professional demand letter citing the specific policy language, line items, and regulatory requirements that support your position.

The Four Negotiation Strategies AI Selects From

  • Full supplement demand — when the gap is large and documentation is complete
  • Partial dispute — when specific line items are disputed but others are accepted
  • Demand re-inspection — when significant damage was missed in the initial inspection
  • Invoke appraisal clause — when negotiation has failed and the gap remains significant

What the AI Letter Contains

The generated demand letter opens with your specific documented gap amount, cites the exact policy sections requiring payment of disputed items, references specific line items from the estimate comparison, sets a 10-business-day response deadline, and closes with reference to regulatory complaint and appraisal rights if the insurer does not respond.

A demand letter that says your estimate is too low gets ignored. A demand letter that says your estimate omits $6,200 in ridge vent replacement and applies a labor rate 22 percent below local market gets a response.
