What AI Estimate Review Does

Claim Command Pro's AI estimate review compares your insurer's estimate to independent contractor pricing line by line. It identifies every item where the insurer's unit price is below market rate, every line item missing from the insurer's scope, every overhead and profit omission, and every depreciation application that does not comply with your policy.

What AI Finds in a Typical Estimate

  • Labor rates 15 to 30 percent below local contractor pricing
  • Overhead and profit omitted on claims requiring a general contractor
  • Missing line items for code-required upgrades like drip edge and ice shield
  • Depreciation applied to non-depreciable items
  • Scope gaps where visible damage was not included in the estimate

How It Works in Claim Command Pro

Upload your insurer's estimate as a PDF.
