Why the Proof of Loss Review Matters

The proof of loss is your sworn legal statement of what you are claiming and for how much. Once submitted, it is difficult to amend without the insurer's cooperation. A deficient proof of loss — missing documentation, understated amounts, or procedural errors — gives the insurer grounds to reject it or use it against you in a dispute.

What AI Proof of Loss Review Checks

  • All required fields are completed — date of loss, cause, property description, claimed amount
  • The claimed amount matches your contractor estimate and inventory totals
  • All supporting documents are listed and attached
  • The submission date is within your policy deadline
  • The claimed amount is not understated relative to your documented loss
  • The language does not inadvertently waive rights or limit future claims

Common Proof of Loss Errors AI Catches

  • Claimed amount lower than contractor estimate total
  • Missing attachment list — documents referenced but not attached
  • Date of loss listed incorrectly
  • Property description too vague to support specific line items
  • Submission after policy deadline

Never submit a proof of loss that understates your actual documented loss. The number you put on the proof of loss becomes the ceiling of your claim. AI ensures you are claiming the full documented amount.
