What AI Denial Analysis Does

Claim Command Pro's AI reads your denial letter and your policy simultaneously. It identifies the specific exclusion or policy provision the insurer cited, checks whether that provision actually applies to your cause of loss, identifies factual errors in the insurer's position, and generates a point-by-point rebuttal citing the correct policy language.

The Most Common Wrongful Denials AI Identifies

  • Exclusion misapplication — citing an exclusion that does not apply to the cause of loss
  • Gradual damage designation — claiming sudden damage was gradual without evidence
  • Maintenance denial — attributing covered storm damage to pre-existing condition
  • Policy interpretation errors — misreading coverage scope or endorsement applicability
  • Procedural denial — citing late notice when notice was timely

What the AI Appeal Contains

The generated appeal letter identifies each basis for denial, disputes it with specific policy language, attaches supporting evidence references, and cites the state claim handling regulation requiring the insurer to pay covered losses. It closes with a deadline and reference to regulatory complaint and appraisal rights.

A denial letter must cite specific policy language. If the cited language does not actually exclude your loss — or if the insurer misapplied it — the denial is appealable. AI identifies that mismatch in minutes.
