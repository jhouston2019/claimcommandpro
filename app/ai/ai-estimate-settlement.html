Why You Need to Know Your Number First

Most policyholders enter settlement negotiations without knowing what their claim is actually worth. They accept the insurer's number because they have no independent basis for comparison. AI settlement estimation gives you that basis — a documented, calculated claim value derived from your policy limits, contractor pricing, and coverage analysis.

What AI Settlement Estimation Calculates

  • Dwelling structure repair or replacement cost from contractor estimate
  • Contents replacement value from inventory
  • Additional living expenses from documented displacement costs
  • Code upgrade costs from ordinance and law coverage
  • Recoverable depreciation if you have RCV coverage
  • O&P calculation if a general contractor is required
  • Deductible offset
  • Net settlement range — minimum acceptable to maximum supportable

How It Works

Input your coverage limits, your contractor's estimate, your contents inventory, and your documented ALE expenses. AI calculates the maximum supportable claim value under your policy, the minimum acceptable settlement based on documented loss, and the recommended opening demand for negotiation.

Knowing your number before you negotiate is the single most important advantage you can have. The insurer already knows what they think your claim is worth. AI closes that information gap.
