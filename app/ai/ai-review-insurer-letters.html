What AI Analysis of an Insurer Letter Produces

Upload the letter as a PDF or paste the text. Insurer letters often contain deadlines buried in legal language. Missing a response deadline can waive your rights entirely. AI identifies every deadline in the letter and adds it to your claim timeline.

Reservation of Rights Letters

A reservation of rights letter means the insurer is investigating coverage while continuing to handle your claim. It does not mean your claim is denied. It means you should respond in writing confirming receipt, continue documenting your loss, and not sign any releases until coverage is confirmed.
