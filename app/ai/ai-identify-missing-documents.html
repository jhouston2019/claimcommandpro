Why Missing Documentation Causes Underpayment

Insurance companies pay claims based on documented evidence. Every dollar you claim must be supported by a corresponding document. When documentation is incomplete, the insurer has grounds to reduce, delay, or deny payment — not because your damage is invalid, but because it is not provable on paper.

What AI Documentation Audit Checks

  • Proof of loss — present, complete, and within deadline
  • Written notice of loss — sent certified mail with documentation
  • Damage photographs — complete coverage of all damaged areas
  • Room-by-room inventory — all personal property documented
  • Independent contractor estimate — line-item format
  • ALE receipts — if displacement occurred
  • Claim diary — all adjuster contacts logged
  • Policy documents — declarations page and all endorsements
  • Response to any insurer letters — denial, reservation of rights, requests
