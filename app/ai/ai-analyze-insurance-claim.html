What Full Claim Analysis Covers

Claim Command Pro's AI claim analysis evaluates every dimension of your claim simultaneously — your policy coverage against your reported loss, your insurer's estimate against contractor pricing, your documentation against professional standards, and your claim timeline against regulatory requirements. The output is a structured analysis identifying every gap and the specific action required to close it.

What the Analysis Produces

  • Coverage confirmation — which policy sections apply to your loss
  • Estimate gap — documented difference between insurer and contractor pricing
  • Documentation gaps — every missing document with deadline status
  • Negotiation position — recommended strategy based on gap size and claim phase
  • Timeline status — upcoming deadlines and regulatory requirements
  • Next actions — prioritized list of what to do in the next 10 business days

How It Works

Complete your claim profile in Claim Command Pro — property details, cause of loss, insurer name, claim phase, and uploaded documents. The AI synthesizes everything and produces a claim analysis report. As your claim progresses, the analysis updates to reflect your current position.
