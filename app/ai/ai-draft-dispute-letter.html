What Makes an Insurance Dispute Letter Effective

Generic dispute letters are ignored. Effective dispute letters cite the specific policy section being disputed, reference the specific line items or coverage determinations being challenged, include the dollar amount being demanded, set a specific response deadline, and reference the regulatory consequences of non-response. AI generates exactly this.

Types of Dispute Letters AI Can Draft

  • Supplement request — adding omitted line items to an existing estimate
  • Low estimate dispute — challenging the insurer's pricing across the full scope
  • Coverage dispute — challenging the insurer's determination that a loss is excluded
  • Depreciation dispute — challenging excessive or improper depreciation application
  • Denial appeal — point-by-point rebuttal of a coverage denial
  • Delay complaint — demanding response within regulatory timeframes
  • Escalation letter — requesting supervisor review after adjuster non-response

What Every AI Dispute Letter Includes

Claim number and claim context at the top. Specific policy section citations. Specific line item references with dollar amounts. A response deadline — 10 business days is standard. Reference to your state's claim handling regulations. Reference to appraisal rights and regulatory complaint options if the insurer does not respond.

The difference between a letter that gets a response and one that gets ignored is specificity. A letter that says your estimate omits $4,200 in overhead and profit per your policy's replacement cost provision gets reviewed. A letter that says your estimate is too low does not.
