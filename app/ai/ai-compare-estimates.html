Why Estimate Comparison Matters

The gap between your contractor's estimate and the insurer's estimate is the financial center of your claim dispute. Every line where the numbers differ is a documented dispute point. Manual comparison of two detailed estimates takes hours and requires expertise to interpret. AI does it in minutes and produces a structured variance report.

What the Comparison Identifies

  • Lines present in the contractor estimate but absent from the insurer's estimate
  • Lines where the insurer's unit price is below the contractor's market rate
  • Lines where quantities differ between the two estimates
  • Overhead and profit omissions
  • Depreciation discrepancies
  • Code upgrade line items missing from the insurer's scope

How It Works

Upload both estimates as PDFs. Claim Command Pro's comparison engine extracts line items from each, matches them by description, and produces a side-by-side variance table. Each row shows the insurer's number, the contractor's number, the dollar difference, and a classification — missing item, pricing gap, quantity discrepancy, or depreciation dispute.

A line-item variance report is the most effective document you can submit with a supplement request. It eliminates ambiguity — every dispute is a specific number on a specific line, not a general complaint about the estimate being low.

What You Get

  • Side-by-side line-item comparison table
  • Total documented gap amount
  • Classification of each variance by type
  • Supplement request letter citing specific line items
