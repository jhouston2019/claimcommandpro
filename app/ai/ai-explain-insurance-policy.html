Why Policies Are Hard to Read

Standard homeowners insurance policies are 40 to 80 pages of legal language written to protect the insurer, not to inform the policyholder. Coverage is defined in one section, modified by endorsements in another, and excluded in a third — with cross-references throughout. Most policyholders have never read their full policy before they need to file a claim.

What AI Policy Explanation Covers

  • What each coverage section pays for in plain language
  • The dollar limit for each coverage
  • What is specifically excluded and why
  • What endorsements you have and what they change
  • Whether you have RCV or ACV settlement
  • What your deductible applies to
  • What ordinance and law coverage means for your renovation costs
  • Whether you have matching coverage and what triggers it
