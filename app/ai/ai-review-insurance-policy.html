What AI Policy Review Does

Claim Command Pro's AI policy analyzer reads your full homeowners insurance policy — including the declarations page, coverage sections, exclusions, and all endorsements — and produces a plain-language coverage summary. It identifies your dwelling limit, deductible, settlement type, ALE coverage, and every endorsement that applies to your claim.

What Most Policyholders Discover

  • Ordinance and law coverage they did not know they had
  • Extended replacement cost endorsements that add 25 to 50 percent above the dwelling limit
  • Matching clauses that require full surface replacement
  • Sublimits on jewelry, electronics, and collectibles
  • Exclusions that do not apply to their cause of loss

How It Works

Upload your policy PDF. The AI extracts text from the document, identifies every coverage section, and produces a structured coverage map showing what is covered, what is excluded, and what endorsements modify the base coverage. It then cross-references your active claim type to flag which coverages are most relevant.

The most valuable coverages in a standard policy — ordinance and law, extended replacement cost, and matching clauses — are also the most commonly ignored by insurance adjusters. AI policy review finds them first.
