What Is a Storm Damage Insurance Claim?

Storm damage is the most common reason homeowners file claims. Here is what is covered, what to document first, and where claims most commonly fall short.

Definition
A storm damage insurance claim is a property claim for physical damage caused by windstorms, hail, lightning, or tornado — covered perils in virtually every standard homeowners policy.

What Storm Damage Covers

  • Wind damage to roof, siding, and structure
  • Hail damage to roofing, siding, gutters, and equipment
  • Lightning strike damage — structure and electrical systems
  • Wind-driven rain intrusion through storm-damaged openings
  • Damage from wind-displaced objects — fallen trees, debris
  • Tornado structural damage

First Steps After a Storm

  • Photograph all damage before any cleanup
  • Begin emergency mitigation — tarp damaged roof, board broken windows
  • Get a certified storm report confirming storm date, type, and intensity
  • File the claim promptly
  • Get a contractor assessment before the adjuster inspection

What Adjusters Most Commonly Miss

  • HVAC condenser damage from hail
  • Gutter damage
  • Fascia and soffit damage
  • Fence damage
  • Outbuilding damage
  • Soft metal damage — vents, flashing, caps

A certified storm report is essential — it establishes the date and severity of the storm at your specific location. Obtain it from a weather service before filing your claim.
