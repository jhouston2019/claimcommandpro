What Is a Supplement in Insurance?

A supplement is how you recover money the adjuster missed. Here is when to file one, what to put in it, and how to make it impossible to ignore.

Definition
An insurance supplement is a formal written request to add previously omitted or newly discovered items to the insurer's estimate after the initial claim inspection and estimate have been issued.

When to File a Supplement

  • The adjuster's estimate omits line items your contractor includes
  • Additional damage is discovered after the initial inspection
  • The adjuster used pricing below current market rates
  • O&P was omitted and a general contractor is required
  • Code upgrade costs were omitted
  • Matching replacement was not included

What a Supplement Must Contain

  • Your claim number and contact information
  • A specific list of items being added with quantities and pricing
  • Documentation supporting each added item — photographs, contractor estimate, code citations
  • A written explanation of why each item is required and was omitted
  • A response deadline — 10 business days is standard

How to Submit It

Submit every supplement in writing via certified mail with return receipt. Send it to your adjuster and copy their supervisor. Email is acceptable as a follow-up but does not create the same legal record. Date your submission — the date matters if the insurer delays response.

A phone call asking about missed items is not a supplement. A written letter with documentation attached and a response deadline is a supplement. Only the written version creates a documented record the insurer must respond to.

What Happens After You Submit

The insurer must review your supplement and respond within the timeframe required by your state's claim handling regulations — typically 10 to 30 days. If they agree, they issue a revised estimate and additional payment. If they disagree, they must provide a written explanation. If they do not respond, that is a regulatory violation you can report to your state's Department of Insurance.
