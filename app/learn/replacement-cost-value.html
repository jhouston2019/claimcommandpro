What Is Replacement Cost Value (RCV)?

The difference between RCV and ACV is often tens of thousands of dollars. Here is what RCV means, how it pays out, and what you need to do to collect it.

Definition
Replacement cost value pays what it actually costs to replace damaged property with new materials of like kind and quality at today's prices — without deducting for depreciation.

RCV vs ACV — The Core Difference

Replacement cost value and actual cash value are the two primary settlement types in property insurance. RCV pays the full cost to repair or replace damaged property with new equivalent materials. ACV pays RCV minus depreciation — the current market value of the damaged item accounting for age and wear. On a 15-year-old roof, the difference between RCV and ACV can be 60 to 80 percent of the total replacement cost.

How RCV Pays Out

Most RCV policies pay in two stages. First, the insurer pays the actual cash value — the depreciated amount. After you complete repairs and submit proof, the insurer releases the depreciation holdback — called recoverable depreciation — bringing the total payment to full replacement cost. You must complete repairs to collect the holdback.

What RCV Covers

  • Structural repairs at current contractor labor and material rates
  • Roof replacement at current shingle and installation pricing
  • Contents replacement at current retail prices for equivalent items
  • Code upgrade costs if ordinance and law coverage is included

If you have RCV coverage and complete repairs, you are entitled to the depreciation holdback. Keep every contractor receipt — you need them to collect the full RCV amount. Submitting proof of completion is required; the insurer will not release the holdback automatically.

How to Confirm Your Settlement Type

Your declarations page lists the settlement type for each coverage. Look for "Replacement Cost," "RCV," or "RC" next to Coverage A, Coverage C, and other coverages. If it says "Actual Cash Value" or "ACV," you are on a depreciated settlement for that coverage.
