What Is a Lightning Damage Insurance Claim?

Lightning damage extends well beyond where it strikes. Here is what is covered — and how power surges affect your claim.

Definition
A lightning damage insurance claim covers physical damage to the insured structure, electrical systems, and personal property caused by a direct lightning strike or by power surges resulting from a strike.

What Lightning Damage Covers

  • Structural fire damage from a lightning strike
  • Electrical system damage — panel, wiring, outlets
  • Appliances and electronics damaged by surge from a direct or nearby strike
  • HVAC system damage
  • Telephone and cable system damage

Direct Strike vs Power Surge

Direct strikes cause structural and electrical damage at the point of contact. Power surges from nearby strikes travel through utility lines and can damage electronics, appliances, and electrical systems throughout the structure — often without any visible point of entry on the building.

Documentation for Lightning Claims

  • Storm report confirming lightning activity at your location on the loss date
  • Electrician assessment of all electrical damage
  • List of all damaged electronics and appliances with make, model, and replacement cost
  • Photographs of all visible damage points

Electronic damage from a nearby lightning surge is covered — the lightning does not need to directly strike your home. A certified storm report confirming lightning activity at your location supports the claim.
