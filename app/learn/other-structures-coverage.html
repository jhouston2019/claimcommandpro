Coverage Terms

What Is Other Structures Coverage (Coverage B)?

Your detached garage, fence, shed, and other outbuildings are covered — but under a separate limit that many policyholders never check.

Definition
Other structures coverage (Coverage B) pays to repair or replace structures on your property that are not attached to the main dwelling — including detached garages, fences, sheds, pools, and driveways.

What Coverage B Covers

  • Detached garage or carport
  • Fences and retaining walls
  • Storage sheds and outbuildings
  • Swimming pools and pool houses
  • Driveways and walkways
  • Guest houses not rented to others

What Coverage B Does Not Cover

  • Structures rented to non-residents — excluded under most standard policies
  • Structures used for business purposes — require separate business coverage
  • Structures that are part of the main dwelling — covered under Coverage A

How the Coverage B Limit Is Set

Coverage B is typically set at 10 percent of your Coverage A (dwelling) limit automatically. On a $300,000 dwelling policy, Coverage B is $30,000. This limit applies to all other structures combined — not per structure. If you have significant detached structures, you may need to increase Coverage B.

Fences are the most commonly missed Coverage B claim. Storm damage to fencing is covered under Coverage B and should be included in any storm damage claim. Many adjusters skip fencing entirely — document it with photographs and include it in your supplement.
