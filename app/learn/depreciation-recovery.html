What Is Depreciation Recovery in Insurance?

If you have RCV coverage and you completed repairs, money is waiting for you. Here is the exact process to collect it.

Definition
Depreciation recovery is the process by which a policyholder with RCV coverage collects the withheld depreciation amount after completing covered repairs and submitting proof to the insurer.

The Recovery Process Step by Step

  • Complete all repairs included in the approved claim scope
  • Collect all invoices from every contractor and supplier
  • Obtain a completion certificate or sign-off from your contractor
  • Write a formal letter to your insurer requesting release of recoverable depreciation
  • Include your claim number, the depreciation amount from your original settlement, and all attached receipts
  • Send via certified mail with return receipt
  • Follow up if no response within 30 days

Documentation Required

  • Itemized contractor invoices for all repair work
  • Proof of payment for each invoice
  • Contractor completion certificate
  • Photos of completed repairs (recommended but not always required)

What If Repairs Cost Less Than the Estimate

If your repairs cost less than the insurer's approved scope, you typically recover only the actual repair cost — not the full estimated amount. The insurer may request invoices to confirm the actual cost before releasing the holdback.

Many policyholders complete repairs and never submit for depreciation recovery — leaving thousands of dollars uncollected. The insurer will not reach out to you. You must initiate the request.
