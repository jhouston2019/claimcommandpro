What is RCV Recovery?

If your policy has Replacement Cost Value (RCV) coverage, your insurer initially pays only the Actual Cash Value (ACV) — which is RCV minus depreciation. Once you complete repairs, you can submit proof to recover the withheld depreciation amount.
