💡 Documentation Tips

List every damaged item with purchase date, original cost, and replacement cost. Include receipts, photos, serial numbers, and model numbers when possible. Most policies have limits on certain items (jewelry, electronics, art) — check your policy for sublimits.

Your insurer will depreciate your items under ACV — your replacement cost entries establish the ceiling for recovery. Enter replacement cost, not what you paid.
