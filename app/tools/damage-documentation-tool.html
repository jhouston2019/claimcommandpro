📷 Documentation Best Practices

  • Take photos from multiple angles (wide, medium, close-up)
  • Include reference objects for scale (ruler, coin, hand)
  • Capture serial numbers and model numbers of damaged items
  • Document the entire area, not just obvious damage
  • Take photos before any cleanup or temporary repairs
  • Use good lighting and focus
