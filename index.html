Questions Before You Start

Is this legal to do without hiring a public adjuster?
Yes. Policyholders have the legal right to document, dispute, and negotiate their own claims. Claim Command Pro gives you the tools and knowledge to do it effectively — no license required.
What if my claim is already partially settled?
You can still recover additional money on most claims. Supplement requests are common and accepted throughout the life of a claim. The Estimate Review Engine will show you if money was left on the table.
Will my insurer take letters I write myself seriously?
The Claim Letter Generator produces documentation-grade correspondence formatted the way claim professionals write it — not casual emails. Insurers respond to what's properly documented.
What if my damage amount is close to my deductible?
Run the free analysis first. If the gap isn't large enough to warrant upgrading, we'll tell you. You've lost nothing.
