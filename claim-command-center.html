Review the Claim Process Guide
Read this first — it explains the entire claim process before you take any action
In Progress
Why This Matters

Most policyholders make costly mistakes in the first 48 hours because they don't know how the process works. An insurance claim is not automatic reimbursement — it is a formal financial review governed by your policy contract. Understanding what to expect before you act protects your rights and gives you the best chance at a full recovery.

What You Do

Read this guide from start to finish before you contact your insurer or sign anything. It explains what the insurance company is looking for, what your responsibilities are as a policyholder, and exactly what Claim Command Pro will help you do at each step.

What Claim Command Pro Provides
  • A plain-language overview of the entire claim process from start to finish
  • How your insurer calculates and pays your claim — and where they can shortchange you
  • The most common ways insurers underpay claims and how to catch them
  • A guide to every tool inside Claim Command Pro and when to use each one

Review Your Policy with AI Policy Analyzer
We read your policy and tell you exactly what you're covered for — in plain language
Pending
Why This Matters

Your insurer knows your policy better than you do — and they use that to their advantage. Most policyholders have coverage they don't know about and rights they never exercise. Before your insurer tells you what they'll pay, you need to know what your policy actually says you're owed.

What You Do

Upload your declarations page and policy documents. Claim Command Pro's AI Policy Analyzer will read every page, extract your coverage limits, deductibles, and endorsements, and flag every coverage trigger that applies to your specific type of loss. You don't need to understand insurance language — we translate it for you.

What Claim Command Pro Provides
  • A plain-language summary of exactly what your policy covers for this type of loss
  • Your exact deductible amounts and coverage limits — clearly labeled
  • Additional coverages attached to your policy that you may not know you have
  • A list of areas where your coverage may fall short and what to do about it

03
Report the Loss Properly
How to report your loss to the insurance company without making costly mistakes
Pending
Why This Matters

What you say when you first report the loss can limit what you're paid — permanently. Vague or poorly worded reports give the insurer grounds to restrict your coverage before the adjuster even arrives. The right notification language locks in your full rights under the policy from day one.

What You Do

Use the loss notification template we provide to report your claim. Do not admit fault, guess at dollar amounts, or describe damage in detail until a full professional inspection is done. We tell you exactly what to say — and exactly what not to say.

What Claim Command Pro Provides
  • A ready-to-use letter template with the right language to protect your full claim rights
  • What to say and what to avoid when communicating with your insurer
  • The most common statements that limit coverage — and how to avoid making them
  • A checklist to confirm your loss report was filed correctly and completely
