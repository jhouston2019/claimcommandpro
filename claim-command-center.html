Document Damage Thoroughly
Create a complete visual record of all damage before repairs begin
Pending
Why This Matters

Your photos and documentation are the only proof you have of what was damaged. Without thorough documentation, the insurer can deny items they claim weren't damaged or weren't part of the loss. Once repairs begin, you lose the ability to prove what was there.

What You Do

Photograph every damaged area from multiple angles. Include wide shots showing the full room or area, medium shots showing specific damage, and close-ups of details. Use our damage documentation tool to organize photos by room and damage type.

Pending
Why This Matters

The insurance company's estimate is often incomplete or underpriced. A licensed contractor's estimate gives you leverage to challenge lowball offers and proves the true cost of repairs. Without it, you have no basis to dispute what the insurer says the work costs.

Pending
Why This Matters

The adjuster's inspection determines what the insurance company will pay. What you say and show during the inspection can either strengthen or weaken your claim.
