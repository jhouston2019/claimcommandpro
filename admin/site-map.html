18-Step Claim Command Center · Core Sequencing
PHASE 1 · ESTABLISH Days 1–7 · Foundation
STEP 01
Claim Process Guide
Informational overview of the entire insurance claim process. Modal-based orientation.
Informational
STEP 02
Policy Analyzer
Upload your policy PDF. AI extracts coverage limits, exclusions, deductibles, endorsements.
GPT-4 PDF.js DOCX
STEP 03
Written Notice Generator
Generate formal notice of loss letter to carrier. GPT-4 drafts legally appropriate language.
GPT-4 PDF DOCX
PHASE 2 · DOCUMENT Days 7–45 · Evidence Building
STEP 04
Damage Documentation Tool
Photo upload + AI quality scoring. Organizes visual evidence by category and room.
GPT-4
STEP 05
Contractor Scope Checklist
AI generates comprehensive repair scope. Flags critical items carriers typically miss.
GPT-4 PDF DOCX
STEP 06
Carrier Request Logger
Track all carrier document requests. Auto-detects overdue items. Client-side, no backend.
Excel
STEP 07
Contents Inventory
Room-by-room personal property inventory tool. Excel export for submission to carrier.
Excel
PHASE 3 · ANALYZE Days 30–60 · Gap Detection
STEP 08
Estimate Review
Upload carrier estimate PDF. AI identifies missing line items, low pricing, excluded categories.
GPT-4 PDF.js DOCX
STEP 09
Pricing Deviation Analyzer
Line-item price comparison against market rates. Quantifies underpayment per trade category.
GPT-4 PDF Excel DOCX
STEP 10
Coverage Gap Detector
Maps policy coverage to claim items. Identifies unclaimed coverage categories and sublimits.
GPT-4 PDF DOCX
PHASE 4 · RECOVER Days 45–90 · Active Recovery
STEP 11
Supplement Letter
AI drafts supplement demand letter incorporating all gaps found in Phases 2–3.
GPT-4 PDF DOCX
STEP 12
Demand Letter
Formal demand letter with legal citations, dollar figures, and carrier response deadline.
GPT-4 PDF DOCX
STEP 13
RCV Recovery Submitter
Submit replacement cost value recovery once repairs complete. File upload + recovery tracking.
PDF Upload
PHASE 5 · RESOLVE Days 60–120 · Final Resolution
STEP 14
Negotiation Strategy
AI generates carrier-specific negotiation playbook with leverage points and counter-arguments.
GPT-4 DOCX
STEP 15
Escalation Evaluator
Assesses when to escalate: appraisal, DOI complaint, attorney, public adjuster.
GPT-4 DOCX
STEP 16
Settlement Review
AI reviews proposed settlement offer against claim value. Flags acceptance risks.
GPT-4 DOCX
STEP 17
Claim Archive Generator
Packages complete claim file into downloadable ZIP archive for records.
ZIP
STEP 18
Release Review
AI reviews final release document before signing. Identifies waiver traps and rights given up.
GPT-4 DOCX
