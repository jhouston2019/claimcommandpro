## USE CASE SCENARIOS

### Scenario 1: Water Damage Claim Denial

**Situation:** Homeowner's $45,000 water damage claim denied due to "gradual damage" exclusion.

**Claim Commander Pro Solution:**
1. **Policy Review:** Identifies sudden vs gradual damage policy language, finds coverage for "sudden and accidental" water damage
2. **Damage Assessment:** Analyzes pattern, confirms acute pipe burst (not gradual), identifies hidden damage risks
3. **Evidence Checker:** Generates checklist for proving sudden damage (plumber report, photos, timeline)
4. **Negotiation Advisor:** Calculates optimal demand ($52,000), provides legal citations for bad faith if denial continues
5. **Response Agent:** Generates appeal letter with legal precedents and causation evidence

**Outcome:** Claim approved for $48,500 (97% of actual damage) after appeal with expert documentation.

### Scenario 2: Lowball Settlement Offer

**Situation:** Carrier offers $18,000 for $35,000 in documented roof damage.

**Claim Commander Pro Solution:**

2. **ROM Estimator:** Validates contractor pricing against market data, confirms $35,000 is reasonable
3. **Negotiation Advisor:** Analyzes position strength (85/100), recommends anchoring at $38,000
4. **Carrier Intelligence:** Identifies carrier's known lowball tactics, provides countermeasures
5. **Response Agent:** Generates demand letter with pricing validation and legal leverage points

**Outcome:** Settlement increased to $32,500 (93% of actual damage) through strategic negotiation.

**Situation:** Fire damage with smoke, water (firefighting), structural, and contents losses totaling $180,000.
